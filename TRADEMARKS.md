# Trademarks and vendor names

The knowledge base names companies and products. This document states the basis for that.

## Nominative use only

Vendor names, product names and trademarks appearing in `kb/` are used **nominatively**: to
identify the company or product a piece of code refers to. That is the only way to say anything
useful about where a dependency sends data.

No affiliation, sponsorship, endorsement, or partnership is claimed or implied by any vendor named
in this repository. All trademarks are the property of their respective owners.

## What we do not do

- We do not redistribute vendor logos, icons, or brand assets.
- We do not use vendor names in this project's own branding or domain names.
- We do not publish evaluative claims about vendors in the data layer. Entries record facts with
  citations; any rating is computed by the published rule table in `docs/METHODOLOGY.md`.

## If you are a vendor named here

If a fact recorded about your company is wrong, `kb/KB_POLICY.md` sets out the process: a factual
correction with a primary source is merged the same day, and the entry is marked `disputed`
immediately on any good-faith objection — before review concludes. That path is deliberately faster
and cheaper than any legal one.
